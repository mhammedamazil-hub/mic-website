Bluetooth Speaker Setup Guide

1
Connect Bluetooth Speaker

Go to your phone or computer's system settings and pair/connect your Bluetooth speaker as you normally do.

2
Grant Microphone Permission

Press Start Microphone in Web Mic and click "Allow" when your browser asks for microphone access.

3
Select Audio Output

If supported by your browser, select your Bluetooth speaker from the dropdown. Otherwise, ensure the speaker is set as your operating system's default audio output.

4
Prevent Feedback & Speak

Keep your phone/microphone pointed away from the speaker, start at a reasonable volume, and speak clearly.

Understanding Bluetooth Latency:

Web Mic processes audio in under 10 milliseconds in the browser. However, Bluetooth hardware wireless codecs (such as SBC, AAC, or A2DP) add an inherent 100–200ms wireless transmission delay. For the lowest physical latency, use a Low Latency Bluetooth codec (aptX LL) or an auxiliary (AUX) audio cable.

Feedback Prevention Tips

Acoustic feedback (the high-pitched screech or echo) happens when microphone audio played through a speaker loops back into the microphone.

  • Distance: Keep at least 6 to 10 feet between your microphone and the speaker.
  • Orientation: Stand behind the speaker or point your phone away from the speaker's front grill.
  • Volume Levels: Start with microphone gain at 80–100% and increase gradually. Avoid maxing out both phone volume and speaker volume at once.
  • Echo Cancellation: If you must stay close to the speaker, enable Echo Cancellation in the Advanced Audio Processing settings.

Privacy & Local Processing Guarantee

"Your microphone audio is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded or recorded by this app."

Our Privacy Principles:

  • Zero Audio Uploads: Audio data never leaves your browser window. No network requests are made during microphone capture.
  • Zero Recording or Storage: No microphone stream is written to IndexedDB, LocalStorage, or disk.
  • No Backend Server: Web Mic is 100% static client-side JavaScript. There is no application server or database.
  • No Accounts or Tracking: No sign-in, cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party analytics scripts.

Open Source & Verifiable:

The entire source code is static HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. You can inspect the network tab in your browser's Developer Tools to verify zero outbound network calls.
